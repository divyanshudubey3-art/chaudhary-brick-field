Brick Grades & Quality Specifications

Baked in coal kilns with standard Indian specifications (IS: 1077)

अव्वल ईंट (Awal No. 1)

Deep burnt, uniform deep red/cherry color, perfectly square edges, zero cracks. Emits a clear metallic ringing sound when struck together.

Compressive Strength > 10.5 N/mm²

Ideal For Multi-floor pillars & exterior walls

Slightly irregular shape with minor hair cracks allowed. Thoroughly baked with high structural integrity. Great balance of strength and budget.
